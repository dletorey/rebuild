Back in February this year I got a message from my great friend, Libby, that they were coming to London in November.

We met at Woolwich and rode the boat along the Thames to Westminster. From there we walk along the north bank and crossed the river via to Southbank Centre.

We went to the Members Lounge and drank some cocktails while the sunset over London. We then traveled to Shoreditch for dinner at [Smoking Goat Thai restaurant](https://www.smokinggoatbar.com/). It's an amazing restaurant and my second visit in a month, I had the Tom Yum Mama Noodles & Tamworth Pork.

Finally we went to [Hidden Grooves cocktail bar](https://virginhotels.com/london-shoreditch/eat-drink/hidden-grooves/), the cocktail bar was music themed and I had Kraftwerk themed Heartbeat - ojo de dios mezcal, botivo, italicus, white vermouth. My friend had (Peter) Gabriel's Cloud which was a Mojito in the form of a cloud.

On the way home a lady got on the tube carrying a Giant Cuddly Banana Cow.
