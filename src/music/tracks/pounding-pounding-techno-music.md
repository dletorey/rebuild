## T-Shirt

The t-shirt I'm talking about is a fluorescent yellow [Soulwax](https://soulwax.com/) t-shirt made specifically for their appearance at [Pukkelpop Festival](https://www.pukkelpop.be/en/). On the front it says _Soulwax_ on the right breast. On the back it reads _Drugs Dirty Dancing & Pounding Pounding Techno Music_.

![The back of a bright yellow t-shirt in black writing are the words: Drugs Dirty Dancing & Pounding Pounding Techno Music](/images/music/tracks/soulwax-t-shirt.jpg)

## Track

The reason that this t-shirt immediately made me by it is because of a track. In 2017 I went with friends to see [Orbital](https://www.orbitalofficial.com/), who had just got back together for the second time, at Standon Calling. When they had finished [Phil Hartnoll](https://www.instagram.com/philhartnoll/) from Orbital was DJing in a wood and he dropped this track that I had not heard before, which was absolutely incredible.

After a bit of research I discovered that it was [Theme From Discotheque](https://www.discogs.com/release/603095-Samantha-Fu-Theme-From-Discotheque) by Samantha Fu. If you search Samantha Fu you'l find that there are no other tracks by them and this is because it is a 2001 side-project from Soulwax.

## Sample

This track contains a sample:

> Do you know where your teenager is at five o' clock in the morning?
The after hours club.
The clubs attract thousands of Chicago area young people.
Some say they come looking drugs, dirty dancing, and pounding, pounding Techno music.

Which came from a news story on [ABC 7 Chicago News](https://abc7chicago.com/) about Club XL after hours club in Chicago which closed in February 2001.
