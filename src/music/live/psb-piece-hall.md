## Hebden Bridge

After a leisurely morning we headed to Hebden Bridge to get some brunch. On the way there we drove past the [Boxford factory](New Works, Burnley Road, Sowerby Bridge, Halifax, West Yorkshire HX6 2TF) that I used to work for in 2003-2004.

Once we parked up in Hebden bridge we found an excellent [Taco place](https://maps.app.goo.gl/i3ZCsCCVVNEFJrwh7) to eat. After food we went for a gentle walk along the canal and found a craft market.

![A canal boat covered in flowers reflected in the water](/images/music/live/psb/tadpole-narrow-boat.jpg)

## Public Service Broadcasting

Public Service Broadcasting were fantastic, they played my favourite song [They Gave Me A Lamp](https://song.link/i/1221477330), from the [Every valley](https://album.link/i/1221474815) album, with an appearance from the singer [Haiku Salut](http://haikusalut.com/).
