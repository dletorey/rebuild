Again, for the fourth time, I got to appear on stage and shout [**More Acid**](/tags/more-acid/) at a crowd of people.

This time it was in London at the [Dublin Castle](https://thedublincastle.com/):

> The Dublin Castle is a pub and live music venue in Camden Town, London. It gained prominence as a venue in the late 1970s after the band **Madness** established a live reputation there. Subsequently, it was an important venue in the early stages of several bands' careers and contributed to the Britpop musical genre. **Amy Winehouse** was a regular visitor to the pub. - <cite>[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Castle,_Camden)</cite>

As it was in London I managed to persuade quite a few of my friends to come and be shouted at.

## Acts

There were 3 acts performing:

- My friend [Steve Hardaker](https://stevehardaker.bandcamp.com/)
- [Sunscreem](https://sunscreem.com/)
- [Filter Sequence](https://filtersequence.bandcamp.com/) (featuring me)

![Me on stage shouting More Acid into a microphone](/images/music/live/more-acid/more-more-more-more-acid.jpg)

We all had a great night. Lots of Dancing, Singing & **Shouting**!

## Cocktails

Once we had finished my friends and I went for cocktails at [El Cenote](https://www.elcenotecamden.com/) a fabulous Mexican restaurant that served fantastic Margaritas of many varieties.
