Went to see [Yard Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yard_Act), despite the [tube strike](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0ljyd03n88o), to go see them on their [_You're Gonna Need A Little Music_](https://album.link/rp7b0wcdvnks8) tour.

Due to the tube strike I chose to drive and parked my car [round the corner](https://www.justpark.com/uk/parking/london/s/236-245-ferndale-road-london-sw9-8ba/) from the venue as we had no idea how we would get home late at night.

## Pigeon

The support act were Pigeon, Afro-disco, grunge, no wave and jazz band from Margate. They were great and quite funky and dancable. They would be amazing at a small festival on a sunny day, such as Bearded Theory.

## Yard Act

Yard Act totally brought the energy and were superb. I did however miss the girls from their [Dream Job tour](/music/live/yard-act-dream-job-tour/), they were certainly missing from Vineyard for the North.

Over all they smashed it and were obviously excited to be performing at [Brixton Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brixton_Academy).
