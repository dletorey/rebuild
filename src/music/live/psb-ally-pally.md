## The Race For Space

This gig was a celebration of 10(+1) years (they missed the 10th anniversary) of [The Race For Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Race_for_Space_(album)) album, released in 2015. It was their largest headline show ever and very special.

![A model of Sputnik flying out above the stage where Public Service Broadcasting are performing.](/images/music/live/psb/sputnik.jpg)

## Meet and Greet

The day after they organiser a _Meet & Greet_ at [The Star of Kings](https://starofkings.co.uk/food-drinks/). As it was my 52nd Public Service Broadcasting gig I took a pack of cards so that they could sign the Aces for me.
