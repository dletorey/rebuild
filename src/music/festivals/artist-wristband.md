## Boho Festival

[Boho Festival](https://www.bohemianevents.co.uk/boho-festival) is a small (about 500 people) intimate weekend festival, just outside Nottingham at [Millview Glamping & Events](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vkcvZgpfxCjMGaQe7).

I arrived at 12:00 on the Friday Lunchtime, once I had received my wristband (more on that soon) I was told to drive into the festival, when I arrived at the entrance to the car park, I was asked if I needed to unload. They then allowed me to drive my car to the campsite to unload and set up my tent, this is definitely not possible at any festival larger than this. Once I'd finished erecting my tent and setting up, I drove my car back to the car park. My car was still less than 50 meters from my tent.

### Wristband

This is the first time that I have officially been an artist at a festival. In 2010 I was on a stage at [Bestival in an Air Guitar Competition](https://flickr.com/photos/dletorey/3931702131/) (where I beat an eight year old child), I won a paint brush.

This time I was officially an artist and performing with [Filter Sequence](https://filtersequence.bandcamp.com/).

![A silver Wristband, around my wrist, that reads Artist Bohemia Friday](/images/more-acid/artist-wristband.jpg)

- Utah Saints - A DJ set they were great and the set list was amazing, I just feel that they need to reduce the number of tracks and play them longer, [Cowgirl by Underworld](https://song.link/i/1444123407) is not 3 minutes long.

## Filter Sequence

This was the third time that I got to perform with [Filter Sequence](https://letorey.co.uk/tags/filter-sequence/). At the beginning I was very nervous but this soon went away when I started dancing to Acid House.

It was a great privilege to be part of Steve's show and I look forward to the next time in Camden at [Dublin Castle](https://eventhorizontickets.co.uk/event/sunscreem-london/), 16 October 2026, when we support [Sunscreem](https://sunscreem.com/).
