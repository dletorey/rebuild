Today is the [first anniversary of my mum's death](/thoughts/mum/). I don't know how I feel or how I am supposed to feel, I dare say that there are no rules around this. I do know that I think about her every day and I think that I always will.

Every time that I open up my phone's contacts, it shows me photo of my mum and this makes me smile, she's saved as one of my favourites, which is there to make it quicker to call people, I'm never going to remove her from that list because she will always be one of my favourites.

![A screenshot of my iPhone's contacts screen, it shows my favourites at the top is Susan followed by my mum](/images/thoughts/mum/mum-favourite.jpg)

Thank you to all those who are think of me, my mum and my family today, I love you all ❤️
