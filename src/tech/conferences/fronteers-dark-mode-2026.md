## Dordrecht Museum

Before the conference Susan & I walked around the streets of Dordrecht and visited the [Dordrecht Museum](https://www.dordrechtsmuseum.nl/english/).

## Fronteers Dark Mode

The conference is called [_Dark Mode_](https://fronteersconf.org/) because it happens in the evening. It started at 4pm, there were 2 talks and then they fed us with a 2 course meal. Then we went back for the remaining talks.

The community was lovely and super welcoming, it's one of the best conferences I have ever attended and I have already purchased my [ticket for next year](https://fronteersconf.org/tickets/).

I even got to be part of the event and was the _Hydration Police Officer_, during Heydon's talk.
